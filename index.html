About the data

    SkimCast is a free skimboarding forecast for 300 beaches, covering every Florida coast and California, plus a few East Coast and Gulf spots. Each beach gets a skim score with shorebreak size, how far out the waves break, tide, wind and the best time to go, updated through the day from NOAA, the National Weather Service and marine forecast models.
